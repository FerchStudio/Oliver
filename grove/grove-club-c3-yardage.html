01The Club

A place to play. A place to stay.

A place to play . A place to stay . Golf here is a morning outside, a shot you did not think you had, and a long lunch with people you like.

1Clubhouse

The Grove Club is a private golf and country club on the edge of Hamburg, set among birches, heath and open sky.

Members play an eighteen-hole walking course, learn at the academy and use the clubhouse as their own. Guests are always welcome to see for themselves.

02Philosophy

Some days are best measured in holes, not hours.

No dress rehearsal and no unnecessary rules. We keep what makes golf good, the care for the course, the respect for each other, the quiet of an early tee time, and leave out the rest.

03The Course

No need to rush. Eighteen holes. No need to rush.

Par 72 | Walking course

Laid into the land rather than carved out of it. Wide where it can be, tight where it should be, and never in a hurry to get you back to the clubhouse.

    05The Clubhouse

    The best part might come after the last putt.

    The 19th hole.

    Golfers have always called the clubhouse the nineteenth hole. Ours is a low building of brick, oak and glass beside the eighteenth green.

    A day at the clubhouse.

    Example day | Concept

    1. 07:30Morning

      First coffee

      Before the first tee time, a quiet table by the window.

    2. 13:00Midday

      Lunch after the round

      Vegetables from the Vierlande, fish from the coast, game in autumn.

    3. 18:30Evening

      The west terrace

      It faces the sunset, which matters more than you might think.

    4. 21:00Late
