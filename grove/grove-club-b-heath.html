(01) The Club

A place to play.
A place to stay.

The Grove Club is a private golf and country club on the edge of Hamburg, set among birches, heath and open sky.

We built it around a simple idea: golf is more than a score. It is a morning outside, a shot you did not think you had, a long lunch with people you like. The game brings you here. Everything else makes you come back.

Members play an eighteen-hole course, learn at the academy, and use the clubhouse as their own. Guests are always welcome to see for themselves.

  • 18 holesA walking course through heath and pine.
  • ClubhouseRestaurant, terrace and lounge.
  • AcademyFrom first swing to lower handicaps.

(02) Philosophy

Some days are best measured in holes, not hours.

No dress rehearsal and no unnecessary rules. We keep what makes golf good, the care for the course, the respect for each other, the quiet of the early tee time, and leave out the rest.

(03) The Course

Eighteen holes.
No need to rush.

A par-72 walking course laid into the land rather than carved out of it. Wide where it can be, tight where it should be, and never in a hurry to get you back.

    (05) The Clubhouse

    The best part might come after the last putt.

    A low building of brick, oak and glass that sits quietly beside the eighteenth green.

    Inside, the restaurant cooks with what the region does well: vegetables from the Vierlande, fish from the coast, game in autumn. The terrace faces west, which matters more than you might think at six in the evening.

    There is no dress code beyond being considerate, and no hurry to leave.

    • Restaurant

      Seasonal and regional

      Lunch after the round, dinner without one. Open to members and their guests.

    • Terrace

      Facing the sunset

      The longest table at the club, and the most popular one from May to September.

    • Lounge

      For the in-between

      A fire in winter, newspapers in the morning, a quiet corner when you need one.
